# 

<p style="font-size:1.25rem;">
We use seismology tools and methods to study active processes on the Earth's surface and are currently focused on mass movements and other slope hazards. We are currently housed in the 
<a href="https://amtseismo.github.io/" 
   target="_blank" 
   rel="noopener noreferrer">Observational Seismology Group @ UC Davis</a>.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.25rem;">
Starting in Summer 2027, our research group will be moving to the 
<a href="https://science.ucalgary.ca/earth" 
   target="_blank" 
   rel="noopener noreferrer">Department of Earth, Energy, and Environment</a> 
at the University of Calgary. <b>I am recruiting graduate students to start in Fall 2027 at the University of Calgary.</b>
</p>

# Current Research Topics

- Debris Flows and Outburst Floods on the Cascade Volcanos 
- Rockfall, Retreating Permafrost, and Climate-related Slope Weathering in the Canadian Arctic
- Earthquakes, Aftershocks, and Earthquake-Triggered Hazards in Yukon and Alaska
